Prototype scope: Functional Web Audio synthesis and routing with eight original lightweight DSP-inspired voice models (Acid, Hybrid, FM, Macro, Wavetable, Resonator, Additive, Cross-Mod), 64 assignable slots, eight bank buses, selectable per-bank insert processing, shared modulation, live keyboard, 16-step sequencer and JSON patch export/import. These are not ports of Open303, Surge XT, Dexed, Plaits, Vital or other third-party code. Those licensed DSP integrations are a later research and implementation stage.
